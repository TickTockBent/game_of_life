Theme previews

Four directions for the new UI, all rendering the live grid. Each has a light/dark toggle.

  • ObservatoryLuminous dots on deep indigo, cool spectral hues per engine. Dark-first; a petri dish under a scope.
  • PlotterGraph paper, hard ink squares, one pen colour per engine with outlined regions. Light-first; blueprint in dark.
  • TerrariumWarm sand and peat, rounded pebble cells in moss/clay/ochre. Soft serif. Organic, not technical.
  • SignalPure black and white cells; ownership is only a coloured bar under each block. Loud type, zero radius.
